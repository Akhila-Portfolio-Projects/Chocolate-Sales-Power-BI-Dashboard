# Chocolate-Sales-Power-BI-Dashboard
Interactive Power BI dashboard for Sales, Profit, and Customer Analysis(2023-2024)
# Project Over View
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze chocolate sales performance across 2023-2024. The dashboard consists of three pages: Sales Analysis, Profit Analysis, and Customer Analysis, providing insights into business performance and customer purchase patterns.
# Tools and Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Power BI Visualzations
# Dashboard 1: Sales Analysis
key performance indicators:
- Total Revenue
- Total quantity sold
- Total orders
- YOY sales growth
- YTD sales
Visualizations:
- YTD sales by country
- Total revenue by category
- Monthly sales trend
- Product, country, and store type Analysis using Decomposition Tree
Interactive filters:
- Country
- Category
- Year
- Month
# Dashboard 2: Profit Analysis
KPI:
- Total profit
- Total quantity sold
- Total profit margin
- YOY profit growth
- YTD profit
Visualizations:
- Total Profit by product group
- YTD profit by brand
- Monthly profit analysis
- Profit by Month and Category
- Key influencers analysis
interactive filters:
- Country
- Category
- Year
- Month
# Dashboard 3: Customer Analysis
KPI:
- Total Customers
- Total quantity sold
- Total Revenue
- Total purchases
- Total Orders
Visualizations:
- Total Revenue by Category
- Revenue by Age Group
- Customers by Loyalty Membership
- Customer Trends by Year
- Revenue by customerId
# Key Insights
The dashboard enables analysis of:
- Sales and performance across different periods.
- Country-wise and Category-wise business performance.
- Product and brand profitability.
- Customer purchasing behaviour and loyalty patterns.
- Trends and performance using interactive Power BI visuals.
